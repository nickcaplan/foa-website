# Slopstrap checklist

Run on every pre-merge gate. List all 17 items as "checked, none" or "found: where". Report tells that already exist on main in a separate line so they are not blamed on the PR. The agent checks rendered pages and visible copy, not only the diff.

1. Pulsing dots or fake live status.
2. Fat coloured left borders on cards or callouts.
3. Shiny buttons (gloss, glow, shimmer).
4. Eyebrow pills, micro-badges and all-caps eyebrow labels.
5. Gradient text, washed-out grey body copy, sparkle decoration.
6. One arbitrary italic serif or coloured word in a sans headline.
7. Marker-style underlines.
8. Glass cards, cards inside cards, spotlight effects, rotating borders, squircle icon tiles.
9. Dark callouts with a stripe, dot and emoji.
10. Bento grids or three identical feature cards.
11. Arbitrary stat counters.
12. Logo marquee.
13. Fake testimonials.
14. Filler copy.
15. Emoji used as decoration.
16. Buzzword headlines.
17. Purple, indigo or cyan neon, glowing orbs, and em dashes in copy.

Pass: none new. A new tell is a finding. Existing tells are listed in `known-items.md`.
